Frequently asked questions

How do I check live arrivals?

Choose a transit system and select a station on the map to see arrival estimates, destinations and service status for supported lines. Taipei Metro also offers an arrival countdown.

Why does the information stay the same when I change stations?

Metro arrival data is cached for about one minute. Selecting another station during this period uses data already retrieved. Once it expires, selecting a station requests fresh data. A ticking countdown does not mean the app makes a network request every second.

What if arrivals are missing or the connection fails?

Check your connection and reload. Provider outages, service hours or request limits may affect results. If a rate-limit message appears, wait for the suggested time before trying again. Missing data does not necessarily mean no trains are running.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your device language preferences. Taipei and Taoyuan Metro station names support Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean. Kaohsiung Metro offers Chinese and English names, with English used for Japanese and Korean preferences. Arrival messages and subscription screens also support Simplified Chinese. Use the menu above to change this website’s language.

How do I manage a subscription, and which currency is used?

Apple handles monthly and annual subscriptions. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Currency follows your App Store country or region, not your interface language. Refer to the purchase screen for current prices and trial eligibility.

Why are location, voice search or arrival alerts unavailable?

Check the app’s location, microphone, speech recognition and notification permissions in system settings. These features require device and system support. You can still use features that do not need a denied permission. Location-based alerts may require background location access.

Arrival times are estimates. Follow station announcements and operator information. Features and coverage vary by platform, device and data source.

Need a hand?

Include the app version, device and operating system, station name and steps to reproduce the issue. Hide personal information in any screenshots.
