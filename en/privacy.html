Understand how information is used.

This policy describes data use in the 捷道地圖 app and this support website. 捷道地圖 is an independent app, not an official app of the transport operators.

01 · Scope

This policy covers the app’s features and support service. Apple, transport data providers and external websites handle information under their own policies. Features and available data vary by platform, device and provider.

02 · Location and route searches

With your permission, the app uses location, which may include precise and background location, for nearby stations, routes and location-based alerts. Depending on the feature, location or origin and destination coordinates, search terms and departure times may be sent to Apple mapping or weather services, or TDX routing services, to provide results. You can change location permissions in system settings; declining access limits related features.

03 · Microphone, speech and notifications

Voice search uses system microphone and speech recognition permissions. The current version requires on-device recognition and uses the recognized text to search for stations; it has no developer server receiving voice recordings. Voice search may be unavailable on unsupported devices. Notifications and arrival alerts use system permissions, which you can disable in settings.

04 · Transit data and third-party services

Queries send network requests to providers such as TDX and Taipei Metro and may include station IDs, routes or journey parameters. Providers receive connection information such as an IP address. Maps, search, weather, Apple purchases and external booking links are subject to their providers’ policies. This version does not integrate third-party advertising tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs.

05 · Subscriptions and payments

Apple StoreKit and the App Store handle subscriptions and payments. The app reads product information, transaction verification and subscription status to deliver purchased services; it does not receive your full card details. Apple handles payment and transaction records under its policy. Use Apple’s management channels for cancellations or refund requests.

06 · Information on your device

The app stores settings, the first launch date and some state used by the notification extension, such as the selected transit system or alert content, on your device. Live query results are cached briefly. System backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Removing the app can remove its local data but does not erase Apple’s transaction records or cancel subscriptions.

07 · Information you send to support

If you email support, we use your email address, issue description and attachments to respond and address the issue. Do not send passwords, full payment information or unnecessary location details. Support information is used for the correspondence and necessary records. Contact us at the address on this page to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided.

08 · This website and external links

This website requires no account and includes no advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Language selection is reflected in the URL rather than stored in a cookie. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and connection logs to deliver pages, protect security and maintain the service; retention is governed by those providers. External websites and email services apply their own policies when you follow a link.

09 · Your choices and policy updates

You can change system permissions, stop using a feature, remove the app and contact us about support information you voluntarily provided. Direct requests about information held by third parties to the relevant provider. Updates to this policy will carry a revised date here; material changes in data use may receive additional notice as appropriate.
